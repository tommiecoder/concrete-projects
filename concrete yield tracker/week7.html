07 / Key Observation

Bitcoin yield is becoming a strategy-design problem.

The interesting question is no longer simply “How much APY does WBTC earn?” It is “Where does the yield come from, how persistent is it, what liquidity supports it, and what risks are required to generate it?”

08 / Data Methodology

How to read this edition

  • Current metrics are labeled Data as of September 22, 2026 beside the metric or in its source cell.
  • Current APY is the current rate shown by the source at capture time.
  • 1D, 7D and 30D APY are measurement windows, not interchangeable labels. The 7D figures shown here are explicitly labeled.
  • Historical APY retains its original date. No historical metric from Weeks 1–6 was changed.
  • “Not publicly verified” means the reviewed primary or credible source did not expose a comparable value at publication time.
  • Sources reviewed: Concrete Earn, Concrete public materials, vaults.fyi, Aave, Morpho, Veda and Lombard primary pages where relevant.
  • This is research context, not an investment recommendation. APY is variable and not guaranteed.
