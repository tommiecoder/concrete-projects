10-year Treasury · 4.79%Concrete USDT · 7.48%

Different risk profiles

DeFi yield may reflect smart-contract, protocol, stablecoin, liquidity, utilization, strategy and operational risks. A Treasury yield reflects a different instrument, market and sovereign-credit framework. Comparing the numbers can provide context; it does not make the risks equivalent.

06 / What Are You Paying For?

The service behind the rate

Use the controls to inspect the operating layers that may sit behind a professionally curated vault. APY alone cannot capture these attributes.

Strategy selection

Selecting a strategy is not the same as selecting the highest visible APY. It can involve liquidity, protocol design, counterparty exposure, capacity and mandate fit.

07 / Methodology

How to read this edition

  • APYs move continuously. Every historical observation in this edition is paired with a source and date.
  • Live APY and 7-day APY are different measurements. A live figure is not a substitute for a trailing-period result.
  • Target APY is not realized APY and should not be treated as a promise.
  • Private APY cannot be ranked. WeETH and WLFIcx are shown as private rather than assigned an implied score.
  • Third-party yield data has its own methodology, coverage and update schedule. Vaults.fyi figures are presented as a dated comparison snapshot.
  • Treasury yield is not directly comparable to DeFi APY. The headline differential is arithmetic context, not a risk-adjusted premium.
  • Historical APYs do not guarantee future returns. Higher APY can accompany higher risk, lower liquidity or different exposures.
