Research Notes

What the comic simplifies

This sequential comic turns a complex product architecture into a visual metaphor: a quiet vault interface above a large operating system below. The underground machine is not a literal technical diagram and should not be read as a complete description of every Concrete deployment.

Examples such as Morpho, Euler, Pendle and RWA represent possible strategy or protocol integrations discussed in the broader ecosystem. They are not necessarily active in every vault.

Factual explanation

  • Concrete’s recent public positioning uses “The Operating System for On-Chain Finance.”
  • Concrete’s public materials discuss strategies, risk management, automation, accounting, capital movement and vault infrastructure.
  • Concrete’s custody-native materials distinguish qualified custody from Concrete infrastructure. Concrete is not the custodian.
  • The layered model includes strategies, risk, liquidity, execution and accounting because those are the operating concerns an institutional vault must address.
