Definitions
VaultA smart contract that accepts deposits, issues shares and deploys assets into supported strategies.
AllocatorThe automated role that decides how a vault’s capital is distributed across approved strategies and when to rebalance.
StrategyA specific route for deploying capital, such as lending, liquidity provision or a hedged position. Risks depend on the route.
ctAssetA tokenized vault share (e.g. ctUSDC) representing a proportional claim on the vault’s assets.
MorphoA lending protocol with isolated markets and curated Morpho Vaults run by independent curators.
LiquidityHow readily a position can be exited without large cost or delay. It can change quickly in stress.
RebalancingMoving capital between strategies to follow the vault’s rules as rates, limits or conditions change.
MorphA Layer-2 network whose Concrete vaults allocate across ecosystem partners such as BulbaSwap and Quokka Lend.
