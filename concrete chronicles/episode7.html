Scene 4 · The accounting engine
Principal+Strategy returns+Rewards
Costs / losses=Vault NAV
“NAV is the value of what the vault holds.”

NAV, or net asset value, represents the value of the assets held by the vault. Returns, rewards, costs and losses all affect that value.

Scene 6 · The reveal
eRate · share-to-asset exchange rate
1 ctUSDC = 1.05 USDC
Illustrative example — not actual performance
“The count stayed still. The value did not.”

eRate determines how much underlying asset each share represents. Illustratively, 100 ctUSDC at 1.05 USDC per share represents 105 USDC. This is not actual performance or a guarantee.

Definitions
VaultA smart contract that accepts deposits, issues shares and deploys assets into supported strategies.
ctAssetA tokenized vault share, such as ctUSDC, representing a portion of a vault.
NAVNet asset value: the value of the assets held by the vault, after relevant costs and losses.
eRateThe exchange rate connecting a vault share to its underlying asset. The share count can stay constant while this value changes.
ERC-4626An Ethereum tokenized-vault standard defining common deposit, withdrawal, share and asset interfaces.
StrategyA supported route for deploying capital. The route and risks depend on the specific vault.
