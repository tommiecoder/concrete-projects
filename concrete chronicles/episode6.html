The Money That Couldn’t Leave the Vault

How AssetCX is designed to connect institutional custody with on-chain utility.

Sequential educational comic·September 17, 2026·Independent educational project
Panel 1 · The number
“We have $100 million.”
“Great. Let’s put it to work.”

Inside a giant institutional vault: $100M in digital assets. The balance is impressive. The operating problem is not solved.

Panel 2 · The problem
“We can’t.”
“Why?”

Institutional assets can be valuable and still be difficult to move into new environments.

Panel 3 · The locked door
“Custody requirements.”
THE ASSET STAYS INSIDE

The custodian points to the giant locked door. It is not a villain. It is the boundary the institution is required to respect.

Panel 4 · The question
“What if the asset doesn’t have to leave?”
“...Go on.”

Concrete looks at the door. Then at the space around it. Sometimes the important design move is changing the route.

Research Notes

Factual explanation

  • Concrete’s public materials describe AssetCX as infrastructure designed to connect custodied assets with Concrete infrastructure and on-chain strategies.
  • Concrete is not the custodian.
  • Specific custody arrangements, permissions, legal scope and eligibility depend on the relevant custodian and product.
  • Concrete / Ceffu September 2026 announcements are used as the primary context for the custody-side example.
  • Yield is not guaranteed.

Based on publicly available information as of September 17, 2026.

What the comic simplifies

The custody-to-AssetCX-to-Concrete-to-on-chain sequence is an educational model, not a complete technical architecture. The comic separates custody from infrastructure so the reader can see the operating roles without implying that assets automatically move, that all custodians support the same workflow, or that any strategy is risk-free.

Panel 11 shows hypothetical custodians. Ceffu is an example of the custody side, not the only possible custodian.
